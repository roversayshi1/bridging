After showing examples of inequalities, I would like to share the rules to be followed
when solving any inequality as part of any Mathematics questions.
Rule Number 1:
If you add, subtract, multiply or divide
any values on one side of the inequality,
the same operation will have to be
applied to the other side of the inequality
sign as well.
Rule Number 2: The act of adding and
subtracting any types of values on both
sides of the inequality will not change the
inequality sign
Rule Number 3: The act of multiplication
and division of positive values on both
sides of the inequality will not change the
inequality sign
