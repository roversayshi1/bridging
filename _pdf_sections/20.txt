Concept of Similarity in Triangles
Both triangles are said to be similar
to each other if and only both
triangle share the same exact shape,
but not necessarily be of the same
size.
When we try to state that two
triangles are similar, we write the
similar triangles in question, followed
by the rule that justifies the
statement.
Example as follows
△ 𝑩𝑪𝑫 is similar to △ 𝑭𝑮𝑯 (By AAA
Similarity Rule)
Implies triangle BCD is similar to triangle FGH
because both triangles share at least two
equal angles.
AAA (Angle-Angle-Angle) Similarity
Two triangles are said to be similar if
