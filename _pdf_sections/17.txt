Typical steps for solving problems that involves uses of quadratic equations
Step 1 Form expressions according to question
Step 2 Form equation as derived from expressions in step 1 according to question
Step 3 Reduce the equation to quadratic form as specified by question
Step 4 Find the solution to the quadratic equation as derived from step 3
Step 5 Reject solutions that are not applicable in context of the question (Negative
Distance Values, Negative Speed Value, Division by Zero and etc.)
Step 6 Answering any remaining questions
Note: Questions in exam condition may sometimes require you to reorder the steps and
think with flexibility, do not feel surprised if the question need you to
• Omit some steps mentioned above
• Repeat certain steps mentioned above
• Add in steps that may seem unrelated to the six steps I mentioned
