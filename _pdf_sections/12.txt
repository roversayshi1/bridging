Several facts to remember about quadratic graphs
• In context of any quadratic graphs, the line intersecting the point vertically where
the graph can be folded into half and remain symmetrical is called the line of
symmetry of graph.
• In any quadratic functions drawn on a graph paper, the point where the line of
symmetry intersects the graph is also called the turning point, minimum or
maximum point of the quadratic function.
