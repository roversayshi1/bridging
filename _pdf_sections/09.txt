Before proceeding on the topic of Maps and Scales, I shall take the chance to explain
why this topic is very important for daily life:
This topic has many applications in real life, ranging from navigation to inference of real-
life distance between two places when all you are being given is a paper map. Even as
smartphone takes over coming with navigation software and hardware installed, this is
still how smartphone can determine the distance from a place to another place. The
map application is set to be able to interpret a certain “zoom” level on the map as a
certain scale on the map application, zooming in on a smartphone map app reduces the
ratio between the literal map distance and actual distance, with zooming out doing the
exact opposite.
